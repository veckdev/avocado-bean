Proteins

The heart of every dish. We source the finest plant proteins — firm tofu, chickpeas, green lentils and black beans — to keep you full and energised all day.

  • Organic firm tofu
  • Chickpeas
  • Green & red lentils
  • Black beans
  • Edamame

Grains & Carbs

Slow-release energy from whole grains and quality carbs. From jasmine rice to sourdough, our carbs are always minimally processed and full of flavour.

  • Jasmine rice
  • Sourdough bread
  • Rolled oats
  • Quinoa
  • Rye bread

Fresh Produce

Seasonal vegetables sourced from Irish organic farms within 48 hours of harvest. No cold storage, no long journeys — just fresh, vibrant produce.

  • Curly kale
  • Baby spinach
  • Cauliflower
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Cucumber & radish

Herbs & Spices

Bold flavour starts with the right seasoning. We include fresh herbs and whole spices in every box — the building blocks of every great plant-based dish.

  • Fresh coriander
  • Turmeric
  • Cumin seeds
  • Smoked paprika
  • Fresh red chilli
